# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Tone & Style
- **Tone**: Engaging, professional, and slightly witty. We want to sound inspiring and helpful without being overly dry or corporate.
- **Hook**: Always start with an attention-grabbing first line (the "hook").
- **Language**: Use active voice, simple language, and write in the second person ("you", "your").
- **Formatting**: Use short paragraphs, bullet points (where appropriate), and emojis to add personality.
- **Call to Action (CTA)**: Every caption must end with a clear CTA (e.g., "Click the link in our bio," "Comment below with your thoughts," "Learn more on our website").
- **Hashtags**: Standardize on 3-5 relevant, high-quality hashtags. Put them at the end with spacing.
